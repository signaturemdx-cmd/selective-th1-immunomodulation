01 — THE UNMET NEED

Suppressing immunity can control disease.
But broad suppression has a cost.

For patients with autoimmune disease, the clinical challenge is not simply to reduce inflammation. It is to control the pathogenic immune response while preserving protective immune function — and to sustain control without an unacceptable treatment burden.

THE PATIENT BURDEN

Long-term treatment can become a lifelong compromise

In autoimmune hepatitis, corticosteroid-based treatment is effective for many patients, but adverse effects, relapse after withdrawal and long-term maintenance create a persistent burden.

THE BIOLOGICAL GAP

Broad immunosuppression does not distinguish the culprit cell

Current approaches can suppress multiple immune functions. A more selective strategy would seek to modulate the disease-driving immune population rather than indiscriminately quieting immunity.

THE DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITY

Selective cell-state modulation is an emerging therapeutic idea

Drug developers are exploring ways to alter pathogenic T-cell activity through cell-surface receptors. The opportunity is to establish a differentiated target, mechanism and therapeutic window.

AIH-specific patient statistics and standard-of-care details should be presented with the final approved clinical references. This page emphasizes the broader biological problem rather than implying that one antibody is already proven to replace steroids.
02 — THE PLATFORM THESIS

AIH is the model.
Activated Th1 biology is the opportunity.

The strategic proposition is not an AIH-only drug. It is a target-led antibody platform intended to modulate activated Th1 cells where the IL-12 → T-bet → IFN-γ axis initiates or sustains tissue injury, with downstream macrophage activation and cytotoxic damage.

The core hypothesis

Target the pathogenic Th1 cell state — not every inflammatory signal in the tissue.

IFN-γ is treated as an effector readout within a defined Th1-initiated biological sequence, not as proof by itself that a disease is Th1-driven. The development question is whether selective modulation of the activated Th1 population can reduce downstream tissue-destructive activity while preserving other immune compartments.

Stage-dependent biology
Platform strategy

Start with AIH. Expand by mechanism, not by disease name.

Each additional indication must demonstrate relevant target expression, an activated Th1-driven disease component, functional response to the candidate antibody and a plausible therapeutic window.

Indication selection filter

Strict Th1-driven biology is the gate.

Do not classify a disease as a fit merely because IFN-γ is detectable. The program should prioritize conditions where IL-12 / T-bet / IFN-γ is an initiating or dominant driver with macrophage activation and/or cytotoxic tissue destruction. Mixed Th1/Th17, antibody-dominant or primarily Th17 diseases should not be presented as core indications.

The disease list is a translational hypothesis framework, not evidence that NT1-1 works in these indications. Disease immunology can vary by patient, stage and tissue. The indication ranking and mechanism statements require disease-specific literature review and experimental confirmation before use in external diligence materials.
05 — CURRENT TREATMENT & MARKET LANDSCAPE

Large and varied markets.
Persistent gaps in disease-directed immune control.

The commercial opportunity is not that every disease lacks treatment. It is that current care often replaces a missing function, manages symptoms, relies on broad immunosuppression, or leaves a subset of patients with inadequate disease control. A Th1-selective antibody would need to prove a distinct role in each disease.

POC · Liver immunology
Autoimmune hepatitis

Current treatment: Prednisolone (or another corticosteroid) plus azathioprine is usual first-line therapy; mycophenolate is used in selected patients who cannot tolerate or do not respond to azathioprine.

Burden / limitations: Long-term immunosuppression is common. Steroids can cause weight gain, Cushingoid changes, diabetes, hypertension, osteoporosis, cataracts and mood effects. Azathioprine can cause GI symptoms, marrow suppression, hepatotoxicity and pancreatitis.

$121.8M2025 value · US, EU4, UK & Japan (7 major markets), not worldwide
Expansion · Endocrine / beta-cell autoimmunity
Type 1 diabetes

Current treatment: Lifelong insulin replacement, increasingly supported by continuous glucose monitoring and automated insulin-delivery systems. Immune-modifying therapies are not a substitute for insulin in established disease.

Burden / limitations: Hypoglycemia remains the principal insulin adverse effect; weight gain, injection-site reactions and treatment burden also matter. The unmet need is preservation of residual beta-cell function and prevention / delay of autoimmune destruction.

$19.3B2025 global insulin market; T1D reported as 78.4% of application segment
Expansion · Thyroid autoimmunity
Hashimoto's thyroiditis

Current treatment: Levothyroxine replacement for hypothyroidism; dose is titrated to thyroid-function tests. There is no established routine therapy that selectively suppresses the autoimmune process itself.

Burden / limitations: Correctly dosed levothyroxine is generally well tolerated. Over-replacement can cause iatrogenic thyrotoxicosis, including atrial fibrillation and bone loss, particularly in older adults. The unmet need is disease modification, not hormone replacement alone.

$1.85B2025 global Hashimoto's thyroiditis drug-market estimate
Expansion · Intestinal autoimmunity
Celiac disease

Current treatment: Strict lifelong gluten-free diet, with dietitian support and correction of nutritional deficiencies. Drug therapy is not an established replacement for dietary gluten exclusion.

Burden / limitations: Lifelong dietary restriction, cross-contamination vigilance, social and economic burden, persistent symptoms in some patients and nutritional deficiencies. Refractory disease requires specialist management; evidence-based drug options remain limited.

$769M2025 global celiac disease treatment-market estimate
Expansion · Skin / melanocyte autoimmunity
Vitiligo

Current treatment: Topical corticosteroids, topical calcineurin inhibitors, topical ruxolitinib for eligible non-segmental disease, and narrow-band UVB / excimer phototherapy.

Burden / limitations: Response can be slow, incomplete or relapse after stopping treatment. Prolonged topical steroid use can cause skin atrophy and striae; phototherapy can cause erythema, dryness or blistering. Systemic immunomodulators carry broader safety concerns.

$1.84B2025 global vitiligo treatment-market estimate
Expansion · Hair-follicle immune privilege
Alopecia areata

Current treatment: Intralesional / topical corticosteroids for limited disease; oral JAK inhibitors for eligible severe disease; other immunosuppressants may be used off-label.

Burden / limitations: Relapse after steroid taper or discontinuation is common. JAK inhibitors require ongoing treatment and monitoring; baricitinib carries boxed warnings for serious infection, malignancy, MACE and thrombosis, based on class / label safety data.

$3.8B2025 global alopecia areata market estimate
Expansion · Biliary autoimmunity
Primary biliary cholangitis

Current treatment: Ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA) is first-line. For inadequate response or intolerance, PPAR agonists such as seladelpar or elafibranor may be considered; pruritus can require separate treatment.

Burden / limitations: UDCA is generally long-term and may cause diarrhea or nausea. Some patients have an inadequate biochemical response; fatigue and pruritus can persist. Advanced disease may progress despite treatment.

$0.68–1.01B2025 global PBC treatment / therapeutics estimates; publisher scope varies

06 — ANTIBODY TECHNOLOGY

NT1-1: a cell-surface receptor strategy for activated Th1 modulation.

The lead program investigates KLRC1 / NKG2A as an immune-cell surface target. The proposed therapeutic direction is to engage the inhibitory receptor pathway — the opposite functional direction from checkpoint blockade intended to enhance anti-tumour immunity.

Research portfolio

Target programs

Separate lead surface-target hypothesis from complementary research areas.

Lead candidate · NT1-1

KLRC1 / NKG2A

KLRC1 encodes NKG2A, an inhibitory immune receptor expressed on selected lymphocyte populations. The research program investigates antibody-mediated engagement of this receptor in relation to Th1-associated IFN-γ output. Target engagement, cell selectivity, downstream signalling and disease relevance require further validation in human systems.

Mechanistic caution: receptor expression enrichment and reduced IFN-γ output do not alone prove that NT1-1 directly activates an inhibitory signalling cascade. The investment deck identifies ITIM / SHP-1/2 signalling and target engagement as work to be demonstrated after investment.
07 — DEVELOPMENT & VALUE CREATION

Prove the human mechanism.
Then prove the disease effect.

The next value inflection points are humanized antibody development, human PBMC-derived activated Th1 testing, IFN-γ suppression and target-engagement studies, followed by mechanism and in vivo validation.

COMPLETED / CURRENT

Mouse-cell ex vivo

Patent-backed target-expression and IFN-γ modulation observations; original data reconciliation required.

POST-INVESTMENT · 01

Humanized antibody

Humanized NT1-1 development, sequence / developability assessment and target-binding characterization.

POST-INVESTMENT · 02

Human PBMC Th1

Activate PBMC-derived human Th1 cells; test antibody-mediated IFN-γ modulation, target engagement and cell selectivity.

POST-INVESTMENT · 03

Mechanism & in vivo

Investigate downstream signalling, including ITIM / SHP-1/2, and test disease-relevant in vivo efficacy and safety.

VALUE DRIVER 01

Human target validation

Develop a humanized antibody and demonstrate target binding, selectivity and IFN-γ modulation in human PBMC-derived activated Th1 cells.

VALUE DRIVER 02

In vivo proof-of-concept

Establish that the ex vivo signal translates into measurable disease-relevant effects in the AIH model.

VALUE DRIVER 03

Multi-indication rationale

Build a disciplined expansion map with disease-specific target biology, biomarker strategy and patient selection.

The mechanism of KLRC1-mediated antibody action has not yet been established. Humanized antibody development, human PBMC-derived Th1 activation, antibody-based IFN-γ inhibition, target engagement and downstream signalling studies are proposed post-investment work packages. These are development plans, not completed results.
