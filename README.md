# Selective Th1 Immunomodulation — Antibody Research Website

Open `index.html` in a modern browser to preview. Static GitHub Pages-ready site with responsive navigation, animated evidence bars, interactive target-program details, and no external JavaScript libraries, tracking scripts, fonts, APIs, databases or client-side secrets.

## Website narrative
1. Unmet need in current autoimmune treatment
2. Activated Th1 platform thesis (IL-12 → T-bet → IFN-γ → macrophage / cytotoxic tissue injury)
3. Patent-backed mouse-cell / ex vivo experimental observations
4. AIH proof-of-concept model and indication-expansion hypotheses
5. Current standard treatments, treatment burdens and selected 2025 global market estimates
6. Antibody technology / target programs
7. Post-investment development plan: humanized antibody, human PBMC-derived activated Th1, IFN-γ modulation, target engagement, mechanism studies and in vivo validation
8. IP and strategic collaboration

## Market data caveat
Third-party market figures are estimates with differing definitions and geographic coverage. They are shown as market context, not a summed TAM or a forecast for this platform. The AIH figure is for seven major markets; the T1D figure is the overall insulin market (the source reports T1D as 78.4% of application revenue); Hashimoto and celiac figures include treatment categories that are not disease-modifying biologics.
